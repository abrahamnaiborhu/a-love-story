## Assignment, purpose and visitors
- Build: new website, starting from an empty folder.
- Subject: a fictional story. A man sees a woman for the first time, wins her, merges with her personality until he loses himself, then loses her and ends as a lonely man: "Mr. Nobody".
- Website purpose and intended outcomes: a showcase of what the Cinematic Web Director skill can produce. Its job is to attract clients by showing that this kind of experience can be built.
- Ambition: client asked for "as extravagant as possible". What "extravagant" means for this audience is still open (see Discovery readiness).
- Visitors: prospective clients judging whether to commission work like this. Device mix not yet discussed.
- Constraints and required existing functionality: React + Vite + TypeScript scaffold (client request). No other technical constraints stated.
- Confirmed facts: the story arc above. All characters are fictional; there are no factual claims to validate.
