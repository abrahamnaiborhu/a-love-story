# Codex Test Batch 01 — Can Codex hold the look?

Status: ready to generate · Written for: Codex (image generation), run by Abraham
Based on: STORYBOARD v0.3, PROJECT_SCRIPT (2026-10-24)

## Why this batch exists

Before generating the whole film, we check three things with four images:

1. **The look:** do the photographs feel cinematic, vague but strong, and like one film?
2. **Consistency:** do he and she look like the same two people in the same world, lit the same way?
3. **The painted style:** which illustration style should the roses and blooms use? Two candidates (03A and 03B) go side by side.

These are tests, not final art. We judge them, adjust the prompts, then generate the rest.

## How to run it

- Paste **each prompt on its own**, as a separate generation. Don't merge them.
- Save the results into `creative/codex/batch-01/` with these names: `01-him.png`, `02-her.png`, `03a-rose-oil.png`, `03b-rose-watercolour.png`. If Codex gives several variations, keep them all (`01-him-v2.png` and so on); seeing variation is useful.
- Tell me when they're in. I'll review them against the storyboard and adjust the prompts.

## Prompt 01 — Him

```
WHERE THIS GOES
This is the man in the opening scene of a scroll-told cinematic website called "A Love Story".
He is the main character. In the scene he walks into night rain and holds an umbrella over a
woman who has none, leaving his own shoulder in the rain. The umbrella and the rain will be
drawn separately on top, so do not include an umbrella in this image. This image is one
cut-out layer that sits in front of a city-street background.

WHAT IT SHOWS
A man standing in heavy night rain, full body, seen from three-quarters behind, so his face is
turned away and never readable. Tall, slim, in a long dark navy wool overcoat, dark trousers,
dark shoes. His right arm is raised, bent at the elbow, the hand closed at about head height
as if gripping an umbrella handle that is not there. His left shoulder and left side are
soaked: darker wet fabric, water running off the sleeve. Head slightly tilted toward his right,
as if looking down at someone beside him. Hair short and wet.

WHY IT LOOKS THIS WAY (THE VIBE)
The whole film is about a man who gives himself away until nothing is left. He should feel
gentle, protective and strong in shape, but anonymous: a silhouette you could project yourself
onto. "Vague but strong" means a clear, readable outline and posture, with the details
(face, texture) dissolved into shadow, rain and softness. His colour in the story is cobalt
blue, so he is lit by cold blue light. The mood is a melancholy film still, like a love song:
tender, quiet, a little sad from the very first frame.

LOOK
Cinematic night photography, 35mm film still, shallow depth of field, fine film grain.
Cold cobalt-blue key light from the upper left, a thin warm amber rim light from behind
picking out the edge of his coat and the raindrops. Visible rain streaks and droplets on his
shoulders. Deep shadows, low-key, high contrast, no faces visible.

TECHNICAL
Portrait orientation, 1024 x 1536. Full body, head to shoes, with a little space above the
raised hand and below the shoes. The man isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with
alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat plain light-grey background with no
shadows, floor or scenery, so he can be cut out cleanly.

AVOID
No umbrella. No visible face or eyes. No text, logos or watermarks. No street, buildings or
floor. No extra people. Not illustrated, not cartoon, not 3D render.
```

## Prompt 02 — Her

```
WHERE THIS GOES
This is the woman in the opening scene of a scroll-told cinematic website called "A Love Story".
She stands alone in night rain without an umbrella until a man walks in and holds his umbrella
over her. Later in the story she becomes a red rose. This image is one cut-out layer that sits
in front of a city-street background, next to the man (a separate image, same lighting).

WHAT IT SHOWS
A young woman standing in heavy night rain, full body, seen from three-quarters behind, so her
face is turned away and never readable. A long, vivid vermilion-red coat, belted, falling below
the knee; dark tights or trousers; simple dark shoes. Shoulder-length dark hair, wet. Shoulders
slightly hunched against the cold, arms close to her body, head a little bowed. Rain hitting
her shoulders and hair.

WHY IT LOOKS THIS WAY (THE VIBE)
In the story she is the only warm thing in a cold blue city: the red the man falls for, and the
red that slowly takes him over. Her red coat should be the strongest colour in the frame.
"Vague but strong" means a clear, beautiful silhouette and posture, with the details dissolved
into shadow and rain, so she stays a little unknowable. The mood is a melancholy film still:
tender, quiet, lonely before he arrives.

LOOK
Cinematic night photography, 35mm film still, shallow depth of field, fine film grain.
The same lighting as the man: cold cobalt-blue key light from the upper left, a thin warm
amber rim light from behind. The red of the coat stays rich and saturated even in the blue
light. Visible rain streaks and droplets. Low-key, high contrast, no faces visible.

TECHNICAL
Portrait orientation, 1024 x 1536. Full body, head to shoes, with a little space above her head
and below the shoes. Isolated on a fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). If
transparency is not possible, use a flat plain light-grey background with no shadows, floor or
scenery.

AVOID
No umbrella. No visible face or eyes. No text, logos or watermarks. No street, buildings or
floor. No extra people. Not illustrated, not cartoon, not 3D render.
```

## Prompt 03A — Her rose, oil-painted style

```
WHERE THIS GOES
This is "her" rose in a scroll-told cinematic website called "A Love Story". The woman in the
story is a red rose. The man is a drop of water who falls through a sky of blooming flowers and
lands in the heart of this rose; she blooms because she drinks him. This image appears at the
moment he lands: the most beautiful, grandest frame of the whole film. It is one layer that
sits over darker photographic and illustrated layers.

WHAT IT SHOWS
A single red rose in full, generous bloom, seen from slightly above, so we look down into its
heart. The petals open outward in soft layers and form a deep cup at the centre, holding a small
clear pool of water that catches the light. Light glows through the thinnest petals from behind,
like a lantern. A short piece of stem with one or two leaves may show below. One or two loose
petals can be drifting near the edges.

WHY IT LOOKS THIS WAY (THE VIBE)
In this film, people are photographs but love is painted: the flowers are a glowing, dreamlike
layer over real life. This rose is love at its peak: warm, overwhelming, luminous, almost too
beautiful, with the faintest hint that it could swallow you. Her colour is vermilion red, and
their love is purple, so the shadows inside the petals lean toward deep crimson and violet.

LOOK
Painted illustration in a rich oil-paint style: soft blended edges, visible but gentle brush
strokes, luminous glazes, glowing light through the petals, deep crimson and violet in the
shadows, warm highlights. Painterly and romantic, not photographic.

TECHNICAL
Square, 1024 x 1024. The rose centred, filling about 80% of the frame. Isolated on a fully
transparent background (PNG with alpha). If transparency is not possible, use a flat plain
black background.

AVOID
No vase. No text, logos or watermarks. No people. Not photographic, not 3D render, not flat
vector, not cartoon.
```
